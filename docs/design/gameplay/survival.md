# Survival

- **Survival is the core of the game:** learn how the world works before it kills you.
- Players manage **oxygen, temperature, pressure, food, gases and liquids**, with simplified physics inspired by Stationeers.
- Players need a base to survive, and each era and planet brings mechanics to figure out.

## Forgiving by design

- **Failures stay contained:** a leak drains one room, a machine stops instead of exploding.
- **Small mistakes never destroy a base or a save.**
- **The base keeps working while players are away;** leaving it for a long time does not make it fall apart.
- The difficulty comes from what the players haven't learned yet, not from punishment.
