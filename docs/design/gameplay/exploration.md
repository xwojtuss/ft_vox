# Exploration and travel

- **Seamless:** no loading screens between the ground, orbit and other planets. Planets curve into spheres as players climb (see [../technical/planet-rendering.md](../technical/planet-rendering.md)).
- **Thrusters** are how players explore and reach new places.
- **Why explore:** unique conditions, life and resources on each planet, and places that only open in certain eras (see [../world/planets.md](../world/planets.md)).

## Warp Drive (late game)

- Jumps a ship instantly to any **warp beacon** the players have placed in the system, at a large energy cost.
- Thrusters stay essential: a place must be reached normally once to put a beacon there.
- Its ultimate use is part of the end goal (see [../progression/end-goal.md](../progression/end-goal.md)).
