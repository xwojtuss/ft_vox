# Building and automation

- **Completely freeform voxel building:** bases, rockets and ships are built however the players want, like Factorio's freedom in how things are laid out.
- **Every block can be shaped:** any building block can be placed as a cube, or other generic types (see [blocks.md](../technical/blocks.md)).
- **Machines and small vehicles are multi-block structures.**
- **Automation exists but stays moderate:** closer to Stationeers than Factorio. Players build and maintain systems rather than endless factories.
- **Room to relax:** players can grow and decorate their base as much as they like, with no time pressure.
