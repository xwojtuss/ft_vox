# Zeniths _(open)_

A star's **zenith** is the time its effect on the system is strongest.

## Current idea

- At its zenith, each star plays with a different force: gravity, electromagnetism, or making atoms unstable.
- This could explain the premise: the expedition's ship crashed while flying through one star's zenith.
- Reaching a zenith safely could need late-game technology and be tied to the end game.

## Open

- Whether zeniths stay a background explanation or become a gameplay mechanic.
- Which star has which force, and what each zenith does to planets and ships.
