# Eras

## What they are

As the three suns move, every planet passes through **eras**: long stretches of heat, cold, darkness, radiation or calm. Which star is close, which is far and how they line up decides the era.

## Rules

- **Eras are like seasons, not disasters.** They change how the game plays and what is possible, but they never make a planet uninhabitable. If animals and plants survive an era, a prepared player can too.
- **The eras are the key, not the lock.** They create opportunities (resources, places, conditions that only exist in some eras) and are part of what makes the end game possible. They are never something the players want to stop.
- **Every planet answers each era differently** (see [planets.md](planets.md)).
- **Eras change the voxel world itself:** frozen lakes become walkable, retreating ice reveals caves, heat dries rivers, snow buries bases.
- **Eras are predictable with knowledge.** Reading and predicting them is part of progression: first they surprise you, then you get warned, then you plan around them.
