# Planets

## Principles

- **No two planets answer the eras the same way.** Each world's atmosphere, surface and adapted life reshape what survival means there.
- **Variety is the point.** Worlds can range from gentle (livable most of the time with some adjustments) to brutal (hard to survive on, but holding the most advanced resources). Some are worth visiting only in their best era, some only in their worst.
- **Planets change colour with the eras,** visible from orbit and in the sky of other planets. Seeing another world change is itself an invitation to visit.
- **Players explore to understand, not only to mine:** what makes each planet unique, and how its life survives, is as important as its resources.
- **Places, resources and dangers appear and disappear with the eras.**

## Shape

Every planet is a flat square map that loops in both directions and is shown as a sphere from above (see [../technical/planet-rendering.md](../technical/planet-rendering.md)).
