# Life

- Flora and fauna on every planet have **adapted to the eras**: they burrow, migrate, hibernate, store food or go dormant.
- **Nature is the players' first teacher.** Watching how life handles an era shows how to survive it, and the players' survival tools mirror those strategies (underground bases, mobile bases, storage and insulation, crops for different eras).
- Before instruments exist, **nature is the first forecast:** animals and plants react before an era changes.
- Fauna also reacts to what the players do (see [../gameplay/threats.md](../gameplay/threats.md)).
