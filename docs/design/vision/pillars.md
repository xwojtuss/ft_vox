# Pillars

Every feature should serve at least one of these and contradict none.

1. **Survival you learn, not survival that punishes.** Danger comes from what the players don't understand yet. Once they understand it, they can handle it. Mistakes cost time and resources, never a whole base or save.
2. **The world changes, and each world changes differently.** The three suns' eras keep every planet alive and shifting, and no two planets respond the same way. The eras are the key, not the lock: they make things possible and are never something the players want to get rid of.
3. **Freedom to build.** Bases, machines, rockets and ships are built however the players want. The game never switches to scripted structures, not even at the end.
4. **Progress you can see.** The research tree shows where you are going from the first minute, like Factorio. The end goal is a real achievement of technology, not a story beat.
5. **Better together.** Systems and expeditions are designed so that dividing the work between friends feels natural and rewarding.
6. **Room to relax.** There is no time limit. Players can build as big and as beautiful as they want at their own pace.
