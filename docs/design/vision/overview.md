# Overview

## In one line

A co-op voxel survival game in a triple star system: three suns reshape every planet in eras, and you learn to survive them, grow from a crash site to interplanetary technology, and finally break out of the system.

## Premise

- Your ship crashed on a planet in a triple star system.
- There is no story, narrator or quest book. **The research tree is the only guide**: its final goal is visible from day one.

## The world

- Three suns circle each other in an endless dance. As they move, every planet passes through **eras**: long stretches of heat, cold, darkness, radiation or calm. See [world/eras.md](../world/eras.md).
- Eras are like seasons, not disasters: they change how you play, but never make a planet uninhabitable.
- No two planets react to the eras the same way, and planets change colour with them, visibly from orbit. See [world/planets.md](../world/planets.md).
- Life has adapted to the eras and is your first teacher. See [world/life.md](../world/life.md).

## Playing

- **Survival first:** oxygen, temperature, pressure, food, gases and liquids with simplified physics, and forgiving failures. See [gameplay/survival.md](../gameplay/survival.md).
- **Freeform voxel building,** including rockets and ships, with moderate automation. See [gameplay/building.md](../gameplay/building.md).
- **Seamless exploration** from the ground to orbit and other planets, then instant travel with the Warp Drive. See [gameplay/exploration.md](../gameplay/exploration.md).
- **Combat as pressure,** never the point. See [gameplay/threats.md](../gameplay/threats.md).
- **Made for co-op,** playable solo. See [gameplay/co-op.md](../gameplay/co-op.md).

## Progression and the end goal

- A Factorio-style research tree, learned one layer at a time. See [progression/research-tree.md](../progression/research-tree.md).
- **To win:** fly a ship of your own design through the Debris Belt at the edge of the system and place the first warp beacon beyond it. See [progression/end-goal.md](../progression/end-goal.md).
- The game continues afterwards, with the rarest resources, final research and room for future DLC. See [progression/after-victory.md](../progression/after-victory.md).
