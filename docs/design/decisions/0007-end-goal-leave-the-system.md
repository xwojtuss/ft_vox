# 0007: End goal: leave the system through the Debris Belt

- **Date:** 2026-09-28
- **Status:** Draft (expected to change)

## Decision

The Warp Drive is late-game tech for instant travel between warp beacons inside the system. To win, players fly a ship of their own design through the Debris Belt at the system's edge and place the first beacon beyond it. The game continues afterwards, with the rarest resources feeding final and infinite research; other star systems are left for DLC. Details: [progression/end-goal.md](../progression/end-goal.md).

## Why

- A real technological achievement built towards all game, like Factorio's rocket and Space Age's edge of the system.
- Needs the best tech from every branch, and uses freeform ships.
- No time limit, and the game continues afterwards.
- Beacons explain naturally why the return trip is safe.

## Rejected alternatives

- **Reaching a place** (e.g. the "Still Point" at the system's centre): you could have gone there long before; not a culmination of progress.
- **Megastructures:** see [0006](0006-freeform-building-no-scripted-structures.md).
- **Stopping or controlling the eras:** see [0003](0003-eras-are-the-key-not-the-lock.md).
- **Special charging mechanics for the Warp Drive** (charging at the centre, near each star, during alignments): overcomplicated.
- **Time-limited goals** (e.g. redirecting a comet before impact): no time limits.
- **Moving the planet, igniting the gas giant:** considered as final research nodes; the Warp Drive was chosen.
