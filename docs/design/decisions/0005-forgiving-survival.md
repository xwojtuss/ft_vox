# 0005: Survival is learnable and forgiving

- **Date:** 2026-09-28
- **Status:** Accepted

## Decision

Survival is the core of the game, with Stationeers-like simplified physics, but failures stay contained, small mistakes never destroy a base or save, and bases keep working while players are away. Details: [gameplay/survival.md](../gameplay/survival.md).

## Why

Players should be challenged by what they haven't learned yet, not punished for one wrong click.

## Rejected alternatives

- **Stationeers-level harshness,** where one mistake can make a whole base explode.
