# 0003: Eras are the key, not the lock

- **Date:** 2026-09-28
- **Status:** Accepted

## Decision

Eras are like seasons: players enjoy them and learn to live with them. The end goal relies on the three stars and never removes the eras.

## Why

If the eras are the enemy, the natural goal is to stop them, but then the most interesting part of the game disappears and the late game becomes boring. A goal that depends on the eras keeps them valuable until the end.

## Rejected alternatives

- **Stabilising the system** (resonators, controlling the stars): the game gets dull as the chaos is removed.
- **Flicking a star out of the system** (triple → stable binary): same problem; also "controlling the stars".
- **Eras as disasters to escape:** see [0002](0002-triple-star-system-with-eras.md).
