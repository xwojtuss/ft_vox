# 0004: No story; the research tree is the only guide

- **Date:** 2026-09-28
- **Status:** Accepted

## Decision

No narrator, cutscenes, lore or quest book. There is at most a one-line premise. Players learn what to do from the research tree, whose final goal is visible from day one and self-explanatory by name, as in Factorio.

## Why

- Stories are expensive and don't fit a sandbox; they also get in the way of replaying.
- A goal players can see and work towards is more satisfying than one they're told about.

## Rejected alternatives

- **Mystery premises** (a previous expedition from the future, ancient builders, a designed system, a signal in the light): need a storyline to make sense.
- **Endings that rely on story,** like Subnautica's.
