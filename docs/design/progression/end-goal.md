# End goal

## Winning

- Late in the research tree, the **Warp Drive** lets players jump instantly, at a large energy cost, to any warp beacon they have placed in the system.
- **To win,** players fly a ship of their own design through the **Debris Belt** at the edge of the system and place the **first warp beacon beyond it**.

## Why it's hard

- The Debris Belt is material the three stars' dance has been throwing outward for billions of years: constant impacts, so ships need shields, armour and defences.
- It is far from all three suns: no solar power and extreme cold, so ships must be self-sufficient.
- **There is no beacon beyond it,** so the Warp Drive can't help: the crossing must be flown the hard way.

## Why returning is easy

Once the beacon is placed, the players warp between home and the edge instantly. The dangerous flight only happens once.

## Inspiration

Factorio: Space Age's journey to the edge of the solar system: a real technological achievement the players have been building towards all game.
