# Research tree

- **Factorio-style:** step by step, one layer at a time, with the next goal always visible.
- **It is the only guide.** Without a story or quest book, the tree tells players what they are working towards; its final goal is visible from day one and its name must explain itself.
- **Reading the sky is part of progress:** from being surprised by eras, to being warned, to planning around them (see [../world/eras.md](../world/eras.md)).
- **The late game leads to the Warp Drive and leaving the system** (see [end-goal.md](end-goal.md)).
