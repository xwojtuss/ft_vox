# Name candidates

Working title: **ft_minecraft**. Check stores and trademarks before choosing.

## Favourites

- **Ternary:** the astronomical term for a three-star system; short and easy to search.
- **Wayward Suns:** hints at the unpredictable eras.
- **Beneath Three Suns:** tells you the setting and the survival feel.
- **Suncrash:** short; fits if the crash stays in the premise.

## Other ideas

- Three suns: Three Suns, Trisol (close to Trisolaris from _The Three-Body Problem_), Threefold, Suns of Three, Third Sun.
- The dance: Stardance, Orbital Waltz, The Long Dance, Dance of Suns.
- Eras: Eras, Solar Eras, Era of Three, Suncycle, Shifting Suns.
- Zenith: Zenith (widely used), Triple Zenith, Zenith Drift.
- World and survival: Under Triple Skies, Castaway Suns.
- The goal: Beyond the Belt, Edge of Three.

## Avoid

- Tristar (TriStar Pictures), Sundance (film festival), Escape Velocity (existing game series).
